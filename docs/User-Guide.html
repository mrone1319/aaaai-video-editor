01 · Highlights

What it can do for you

It's not about having lots of features. It's that every step is checked for you: cuts wait for words to finish, captions match the actual voice, cards stay off your face, and loudness hits platform standards.

Auto rough cut that never clips words

It cuts out silent gaps, waits for each word to trail off before cutting, and starts each segment just before you speak. Restarts and "um/uh" moments are listed for you. Nothing is deleted until you say yes.

Captions matched to your real voice

Each line lines up with the exact moment it's spoken. Chinese, Chinese-English, or original language plus Chinese translation. Keywords get highlighted. If you sit low in the frame, captions move below your chin so they don't cover your face.

Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds

It copies the strongest line in your video to the very start as a teaser. Not sure which line? It scores lines on numbers, contrast, and questions, and suggests 3.

20+ card types that avoid your face

Title cards, animated stats, lists, quotes, comparisons, name tags, chapters, and more. Once they land, they "breathe" gently, and key cards give a quick flash. If you sit high in the frame, cards move out of the way of your eyes.

Card takeover: a full layout for showing proof

The video shrinks into a card while your head pops out of the frame. Above it go proof screenshots, workflow steps, and a call to action, ending on a "comment this keyword" prompt. Great for showing earnings or results.

Audio, all in one go

Noise reduction (you hear three levels first), music that ducks automatically under your voice, 7 sound effects (coin, pop, whoosh…), and final loudness locked to −14 LUFS (the standard across platforms).

Three sources for B-roll

Your own footage is best. It can also find free stock footage (Pexels) or generate clips with AI. Before generating, it always gives you a price quote and only spends money once you agree. Default cap is $2 per project.

Image cards: turn cards into posts

The stat, list, and quote cards from your video can be exported as still images (1080×1350 by default). Add a caption and you have a Xiaohongshu or Instagram carousel post that drives traffic to and from your video. Just say "make image cards."

Every style has its own typography

Each of the 9 styles has its own font personality: bold headings, wide-spaced uppercase labels, monospaced numbers. Backgrounds have a top highlight and soft shadow. Units on stat cards are a size smaller, and you can add a bar showing the amount.

It checks its own work before delivery

It grabs frames at each card's timing to check them, runs a caption timing check, and checks that the thumbnail respects a 15% safe zone. It won't say "done" until it has looked at the actual frames.

Write the posts right after editing

Hand it off to the Content Pipeline: it writes captions and makes images for the platforms you pick (Xiaohongshu, Douyin, X, Instagram, Bilibili…), bundles them into a ready-to-post package, and remembers each client's brand voice. You press "post" yourself.

Fast and safe

It only decodes the parts it needs, so a 60-second video previews in about 30 seconds. Your original footage is never deleted, changed, or moved. API keys are stored in the Mac Keychain and only sent to official addresses.

03 · Walkthrough

Your first edit, start to finish

We'll use a 2-minute talking-head video shot on a phone. Each step shows "what you do" and "what it does," so you won't get lost.

01

Get your footage readyAbout 1 min

You do
  • Put your video anywhere you can find it (for example, your Movies folder).
  • Put any photos, screenshots, logos, or your own B-roll in the same folder. You'll tell it where in a moment.
It does
  • Creates a new project in "Movies › aaaai-editor." Your original footage is only read, never moved or changed.
02

Say what you want30 sec

Type /aaaai-editor in Claude Code, followed by one sentence. Want to be more detailed? Use the prompt template in the next chapter.

/aaaai-editor edit my video

That's the shortest version. It will ask you to drag your video into the window, then ask questions one at a time. The more you tell it up front, the less back-and-forth:

/aaaai-editor Edit ~/Movies/raw/0921.mp4 into a vertical short with Chinese-English captions
TIPDrag the video file from Finder straight into the Claude Code input box, and the path fills in automatically. No typing needed.
03

Answer three kickoff questions1 min

It asks
  • Style: it sends you a style comparison image first.
  • Music: it suggests 1–2 of its 7 original built-in tracks, or you can use your own music, or skip music.
  • Captions: Chinese / Chinese-English / none (for English videos: original language / with Chinese translation).
  • If your footage is horizontal, it also asks: keep it horizontal, or crop to vertical?
You do
  • Pick one. Not sure? Pick the one marked "Recommended."
  • Next time, say "same as last time" to reuse your previous settings.
04

Check the captions3–5 min · The most important step

It does
  • Turns speech into text, fixes common typos, and matches each line to your real voice.
  • For names, brands, or coin names it isn't sure about, it clips that bit so you can listen.
  • Gives you a roughly one-minute caption preview to check timing.
You do
  • Fix any typos (especially names and terms).
  • For bilingual captions, check that the English sounds natural.
  • Tell it which words to highlight, like brand names or key numbers.
TIPThe sooner you lock the captions, the easier everything else is. Card timing is based on the captions.
05

Clean it up, pick the opener2 min

It does
  • Lists every "um/uh" and every restart (it lists them but doesn't delete them).
  • Suggests 3 lines that would work as an opener.
You do
  • Say "delete all" or "delete 1 and 3."
  • Pick one line as the opening teaser, or say "no teaser."
06

Add cards, watch the preview5–10 min · May take a few rounds

It does
  • First writes one sentence on "what viewers should remember," then lays out cards to match the content (one idea per card).
  • Renders a half-resolution preview and grabs frames to check that nothing covers your face or overflows a card.
  • Sends you the preview file and reveals it in Finder.
You do
  • Just say what to change: "The card at 0:20 stays up too long," "Switch the list to a numbered card," "Add a name tag here."
  • Move on once you're happy.
Heads upThe preview final_preview.mp4 is only for checking layout. It's half quality and not the final video.
Want to do it yourself?Say "open the editor," and Claude opens Studio alongside the chat. Like editing software, it has a preview, a properties panel, and a multi-track timeline. Drag cards and captions to change their timing and length, and edit any text or number. Delete a sentence in the "Transcript" and that part gets cut, with later cards shifting forward automatically. "Check-up" marks timing issues, captions that are too long, and cards covering your face as red dots. "Render this section" shows you the real final frames in seconds. Changes save automatically, and anything you ask Claude to change in chat syncs over too, so neither side overwrites the other. Studio only opens on your own computer.
07

Listen to the audio2 min

It plays you
  • Music A/B: the same section as MP3s at three volume levels.
  • Noise reduction A/B (when there's AC or fan noise): original / standard / strong. Standard is on by default.
  • Sound effects: only added if you ask for them. It plays them for you during the preview.
You do
  • Pick one: "music option two," "use strong noise reduction."
08

Export the video and thumbnailA few min (depends on length)

It does
  • Renders the final video: loudness at −14 LUFS, true peak no higher than −1 dB.
  • Grabs frames from the final video to check again, and runs a caption timing check.
  • Gives you a sheet of thumbnail candidate frames (clean shots from the original, no captions) and recommends a few where you're looking at the camera.
  • Makes the thumbnail (vertical 1080×1920 and 1080×1350; horizontal videos get 1920×1080): small label, big title, third line, and your account name, with a dark shadow behind the text. It checks that text stays in the safe zone and off your face.
  • Copies the video and thumbnail to "Movies › aaaai-editor › 成品."
You do
  • Pick a background frame by its number.
  • Give the thumbnail a short hook (a few words), or let it suggest the label, title, and third line.
  • Go through the checklist in Chapter 12.
09

Publish (optional)Depends on platform

It asks whether to hand off to the Content Pipeline, which writes captions, makes images, and packages everything for the platforms you choose. Just say "Yes, for Xiaohongshu and Douyin." The video, thumbnail, and transcript are passed along automatically. You press "post" yourself, because platforms don't allow AI to post on its own. See Chapter 6 for details.

How to fill in each line

[Footage]

The video path. Drag the file from Finder into Claude Code and the path appears. List everything: multiple clips, photos, screenshots, logos, your own B-roll.

[Type]

Talking head, interview, or live event, plus the platforms you'll post to. The platform sets the aspect ratio, pacing, and cover size.

[Core]

The one line viewers should remember. Cards, the opening, and the cover all build around it. Writing it makes the final video more focused.

[Style]

A style name, or "your pick." The style sets the colors and fonts for cards and subtitles (Chapter 8).

[Subtitles]

Chinese / bilingual Chinese-English / original language + Chinese / none. Which words to highlight. Whether subtitles should be bigger or higher.

[Opening]

Pick the line to use as the opening teaser, or let it suggest some. "Add an opening title: …" goes here too.

[Edit]

Cut pauses (done by default), filler words, and flubbed takes. Say which parts to keep and which to cut.

[Intro]

Your name, your role (one line is enough), and where your headshot is. With a photo you get an avatar card; without one, a name bar. See Chapter 5 for how to prepare the photo.

[Cards]

Just describe the moment with "when I talk about…". No exact seconds needed. For common phrasing, see "Say it like this" under each component in Chapter 7.

[Proof]

Use this when you have screenshots of income, dashboards, orders, or reviews. The screenshots must be real. It won't make up data for you.

[Audio]

Which music track and how loud. Noise reduction. Whether you want sound effects, and which ones.

[Ending]

End title, call to action (comment keyword, follow, DM), and whether to hold on the card until the end.

[Publish]

Whether to also write captions and package everything after the edit: which platforms, and how many posts each. If you skip it, it will ask you when the edit is done.

[Other]

Max length, lines that must stay in, proper nouns that are easy to mishear, your account name (bottom-right of the cover).

06 · Publish

Captions and posting: the content pipeline

Once the video is cut, the next question is what to write and which images to use. The suite's social-content-pipeline takes the platforms you name and bundles your video, cover, and captions into a ready-to-post pack. You just open the platform, paste, and hit Post.

The five things it does

01

Gather info

It pulls facts from your transcript first. Then it checks recent news, what similar accounts are posting, and any trends worth riding.

02

Find the angle

It picks the hook, the target audience (a specific group of people), and the tone.

03

Write per platform

Each platform gets its own post-ready caption, with length and hashtags that follow that platform's norms. Numbers and facts come from your content. Nothing is made up.

04

Add visuals

If Canva is connected, it can make images there (it asks first). Without Canva it still delivers: it uses the cover aaaai-editor made, or gives you a clear image brief. Short-video platforms just use the finished video.

05

Pack and archive

Every post comes with its text plus where the image or video goes. It also saves a copy to an archive, so it won't repeat topics next time.

Two ways to start

A · Right after the edit (most common)
  • After delivering the video, it asks: "Want to hand this to the content pipeline?"
  • You reply: "Yes, one post each for Xiaohongshu and Douyin."
  • The video, cover, transcript, and style carry over automatically. No need to send them again.
B · On its own (you already have a video, or just need captions)
  • Start with one of the fixed openers: 启动内容生产线：, /content-pipeline, or content pipeline:.
  • Only these three openers start it. Saying "write me a tweet" just gets you one caption, not the full process.

09 · Sound

Music and sound effects

Everything is original, so there are no copyright issues. Music ducks automatically under your voice. Sound effects are mixed in before loudness is locked, so the final loudness stays accurate. Sound effects are off by default. 3 to 6 per minute is plenty; more gets noisy.

Studio opens next to Claude: preview on the left, properties and tools on the right, and a multi-track timeline below. Every change saves to the edit sheet automatically. 15 · Good habits

Habits for consistent videos

Lock captions first, then place cardsCard timing follows the captions. Change captions later and the cards may need to move too.
One idea per cardFewer cards is better. At most one main card every 8–15 seconds. For videos under 30 seconds: opener + one main card + ending.
Leave room when you shootSit in the middle of the frame with a little space above your head. Cards, text-behind-subject and card takeovers all look better.
Tell it proper nouns up frontPut brand names, people's names and coin names under [Other]. That fixes most typos.
A preview is not the finalThe final video is final.mp4, and you only get it when you say "render the final". Previews are for layout only.
Same series, same settingsSay "same as last time" to reuse the last video's settings. That keeps style and volume consistent across episodes.
Use real proofEarnings, dashboard and order screenshots must be real. It only handles layout. It won't make up numbers for you.
It asks before spending moneyAI-generated B-roll always comes with a quote first. Decide after you see it. If you say no, nothing is spent.
